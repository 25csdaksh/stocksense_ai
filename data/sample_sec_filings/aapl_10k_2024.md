# Apple Inc. 10-K Excerpt (FY 2024)
## Item 1A. Risk Factors

Global Operations and Sourcing:
The Company's business can be impacted by international trade disputes, tariffs, and geopolitical events. Substantially all of the Company's manufacturing is performed by outsourced partners concentrated in Asia. Any disruption to advanced silicon fabrication facilities or logistics hubs could materially affect product shipments and operating gross margins.

Services and Platform Ecosystem:
The Company faces intense competition in its digital services and App Store platform. Legal and regulatory actions regarding application distribution models and commission structures could reduce revenues from Services.

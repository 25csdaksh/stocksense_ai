# NVIDIA Corporation 10-K Excerpt (FY 2024)
## Item 1A. Risk Factors

Advanced Semiconductor Packaging and Foundry Dependencies:
We rely on third-party foundries, primarily TSMC, to manufacture all of our semiconductor wafers and advanced CoWoS (Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate) packaging. Failure to secure sufficient wafer allocations or packaging substrate to meet datacenter demand could limit revenue growth.

Export Controls and Geopolitical Shifts:
Changes in United States export administration regulations restricting high-performance compute accelerators to specific geographic jurisdictions could adversely affect our global addressable datacenter market.

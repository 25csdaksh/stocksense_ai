# Microsoft Corporation Earnings Call Transcript — Q4 FY 2024

Satya Nadella — Chairman & CEO:
"We had a strong close to the fiscal year, with Microsoft Cloud revenue reaching $36.8 billion, up 21% year-over-year. Azure expanded market share as customers migrated general-purpose workloads and accelerated their AI transformations. Over 60,000 customers now use Azure AI, up nearly 60% year-over-year."

Amy Hood — CFO:
"Capital expenditures in Q4 were $19.0 billion to support cloud and AI demand. Approximately half of this cloud spend was for long-lived assets that will support monetization over the next 15 years and beyond, with the remaining spend on servers (both CPUs and GPUs) based on customer demand signals."
